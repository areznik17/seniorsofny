Questions families ask

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Clinton County?

Start with Clinton County Office for the Aging at 518-565-4620. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Clinton County Department of Social Services at 518-565-3300.

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Essex County?

Start with Essex County Office for the Aging at 518-873-3695. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Essex County Department of Social Services at 518-873-3441.

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Franklin County?

Start with Franklin County Office for the Aging at 518-481-1526. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Franklin County Department of Social Services at 518-481-1888.

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Hamilton County?

Start with Warren/Hamilton Counties Office for the Aging at 518-761-6347. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Hamilton County Department of Social Services at 518-648-6131.

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Jefferson County?

Start with Jefferson County Office for the Aging at 315-785-3191. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Jefferson County Department of Social Services at 315-785-3000.

Who do I call to get help for my mom or dad in Lewis County?

Start with Lewis County Office for the Aging at 315-376-5313. For Medicaid, SNAP or HEAP, call Lewis County Department of Social Services at 315-376-5400.

Not sure which office?NY Connects can point you to the right place for your parent's address: 1-800-342-9871.
